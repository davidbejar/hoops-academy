# hoops_finance_api (EXPERIMENTAL)
An academy's finance (ADR-0051 §2, ADR-0056): fee plans, the dues they create per player and period, payments
recorded by hand, expenses, a season balance and CSV exports. No payment provider; nothing is collected online.
Served by finance-service (Go, ADR-0055) under `/api/finance/_**`; tokens need the audience `finance-service`.

Money (decision 94): one ISO 4217 currency per academy (`FinanceSettings`), chosen by ADMIN, fixed once the first
due exists. Every amount is an integer in that currency's minor unit (`amountMinor`; EUR cents, IDR sen, JPY yen);
`minorDigits` says how many decimals the minor unit has. No conversion.

Periods (decision 93): each fee plan has a cadence. MONTHLY periods are calendar months (`2026-09`), QUARTERLY
periods calendar quarters (`2026-Q3` = July to September), a SEASON plan has one period `SEASON` whose dates are
the season's. `firstPeriod` / `lastPeriod` bound a plan's periods (none for SEASON; no `lastPeriod` = to the end of
the season); a period outside its season creates no dues.

Dues (decisions 95, 97, 98): one due per player, plan and period, for every period in which the player is on the
roster of the plan's team (or of a team of its age category) on any day, at the plan's full amount (no
proration). A daily job creates them for plans with `autoGenerate`; ADMIN can also generate a plan's dues for a
period by hand (idempotent, with a preview). States: OPEN → PAID (one payment for the whole amount) or WAIVED
(ADMIN, with a reason). While OPEN, ADMIN may change the amount with a reason. A wrong payment is voided with a
reason: it is kept as voided and the due is OPEN again. No partial payments, no refunds.

Expenses (decision 99): a fixed list of categories (`ExpenseCategory`), localised by the web; no management of
categories.

Reasons and notes (privacy P-12): short free text (at most 200 characters) that must hold no health or family
details and no salary of a named person; ADMIN reads them, families never see them; they are never written to logs,
traces or audit metadata.

Authorization (decision 96, docs/domain/authz-matrix.md section Finance): ADMIN manages everything. A team's
current COACH reads who of the team is up to date (status only: no amounts, no expenses, no balance). A PARENT
reads the dues and payments of linked players, a PLAYER their own (`listPlayerDues`). ASSISTANT_COACH has no
access. A member without the role gets 403, a non-member of the academy 404.

Build state of 1.0.0 (WP118): settings and fee plans are served; every other operation answers **501** Problem
with `code` = `NOT_IMPLEMENTED` until its work package lands (dues and payments: WP119; expenses, balance and
exports: WP123). The contract does not change when they do.
