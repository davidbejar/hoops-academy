# Parent guide

Languages: [Español](parent.es.md) · English · [Bahasa Indonesia](parent.id.md)

You sign in with your e-mail address and your password.

You have the **Parent** role (called "Familia" in Spanish). You see and act only on the players an administrator linked
to you. Bottom bar: **Matches**, **Training**, **My players**. **Profile** is in the top bar (language, sign out).

If you see "Your account does not belong to any academy yet", ask the academy administrator to invite you. If **My players**
says "You have no linked players", ask the administrator to link your child to your account.

## 1. My players
**My players** shows one child at a time; with several children use the child switcher. For the selected child you get:
- **Season statistics** (points, rebounds, assists, minutes, per-game averages).
- **Upcoming training** (next 14 days, for the child's teams).
- **Plans**: assigned training plans with their completion state.
- Health tabs: **Injuries**, **Growth**, **Physical tests**, and an availability badge (Available / Injured).

## 2. Entering stats for your child during a match
1. **Matches**, open a live match (status **Live**).
2. The chip **"Family mode: your players only"** is shown. Only your linked children can be tapped on the court or the
   bench; there is no clock, period, opponent score, timeout or substitution control.
3. Tap your child, then the action: 2 PT / 3 PT / FT made or missed, Assist, rebounds, Steal, Block, Turnover or a foul.
4. **Undo** voids your last play. You can **Void** only plays you recorded yourself. A coach's play cannot be voided by you.
5. Each play shows **Pending**, **Sending**, **Synced** or **Rejected**. Without network the plays wait on the device and
   the banner **"N unsynced"** appears; it clears by itself when the connection is back. If it says "Sign in again to
   sync; your data is kept", sign in again.
After the match is finished, your plays are accepted only if you recorded them before it finished.
The **Box score** tab shows only your children's lines plus team totals.

## 3. Absences
**Training > Absences > New absence**: pick the child, **From**, **To** and an optional reason. Coaches then see the child
as "Planned absence" on the attendance sheet. Delete an absence from the same list if plans change.

## 4. Marking training plans
In **My players > Plans** pick an assigned plan and mark **Done**, **Partial** or **Not done** (optional comment).
Coaches see the same marks. Plans themselves are read-only for you; the **Training** tab shows the calendar of your
child's teams.

## 5. Injuries and measurements
In **My players** open **Injuries**: **Record injury** (body part, severity, date, expected return) and **Mark recovered**.
Under **Growth** use **Add measurement** (height, weight, one per day). **Physical tests** are read-only for parents and show
the expected band when a norm exists ("Reference pending" marks provisional norms).

## Privacy in short
You never see other families' children, their stats or their health data. Anything outside your linked players answers
"We could not find what you are looking for".
