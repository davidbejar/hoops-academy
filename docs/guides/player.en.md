# Player guide

Languages: [Español](player.es.md) · English · [Bahasa Indonesia](player.id.md)

You have the **Player** role. Bottom bar: **Matches**, **Training**, **Me**. **Profile** is in the top bar (language,
sign out). If **Me** says "Your account is not linked to a player yet", ask your academy administrator to link it.

## Me (My sports profile)
- **Season statistics**: points, rebounds, assists, minutes and per-game averages. Choose another season with the season
  selector.
- **Upcoming training**: your teams' sessions in the next 14 days.
- Tabs **Training** and **Plans**, plus your health tabs (injuries, growth, physical tests).
- A badge shows whether you are **Available** or **Injured**.

## Matches
**Matches** lists your team's games with status (Scheduled, Live, Finished, Cancelled). You can open a live match and follow
the score, the plays and the box score ("Read only"). You cannot enter statistics: ask a coach or a linked parent.
The box score shows team totals; your own numbers are under Me > Season statistics.

## Training and plans
**Training** shows the week calendar of your teams (previous, next, today). Open a session for place, time and plan.
In **Me > Plans** you see the plans assigned to you with due dates and exercises (video links open in your browser). Mark
each plan **Done**, **Partial** or **Not done**, with an optional comment. Coaches see your marks.

## Absences
**Training > Absences > New absence**: **From**, **To** and an optional reason. Coaches see you as "Planned absence" on the
attendance sheet. You can delete an absence you created if plans change.

## Health and growth
Under your health tabs you can read your **Injuries**, your **Growth** (height and weight charts, or "Show table") and your
**Physical tests** with the expected band for your age and gender. These are read-only for players. If a coach records a
test and the norm is a placeholder you will see "Reference pending".

## If something does not load
"Cannot reach the server" or the offline banner means the network is down; data already opened stays visible. "Your session
has expired" means sign in again.
